Cosmic Cliffs in the Carina Nebula

April 5, 2026 — Astronomy Picture of the Day
This landscape of "mountains" and "valleys" speckled with glittering stars is actually the edge of a nearby stellar nursery called NGC 3324 in the Carina Nebula. Captured in infrared light, this image reveals for the first time previously invisible areas of star birth. The "cliffs" are about 7 light-years high.
